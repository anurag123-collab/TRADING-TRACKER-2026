Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026

🛡️ Our Privacy Commitment: At The Trading Tracker (accessible via https://thetradingtracker.com), operated by Trading Tracker Technologies (Government of India MSME Registration: UDYAM-BR-24-0058008) and founded by Anurag Patel, we prioritize your personal and financial data privacy above all. This Privacy Policy details the types of information collected, logged, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We respect the confidentiality of your financial journaling. The data we collect depends on how you interact with our platform:

  • Client-Side Journal Records: All trade entries, setup rules, notes, P&L numbers, and screenshots entered inside the journal are primarily stored locally within your browser's encrypted LocalStorage or IndexedDB sandbox.
  • Account & Profile Information: When you register, verify via OTP, or upgrade to VIP Pro, we collect basic contact credentials including your Name, Mobile Number, and Email Address.
  • Transaction & Billing Identifiers: Payment verifications are handled through certified third-party payment gateways (Razorpay/UPI). We never store sensitive banking credentials, card CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers.
  • Technical & Log Data: Like most web platforms, our servers automatically log standard web requests, including IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and operating system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for legitimate platform purposes:

  • To render your customized trading analytics, win rates, and interactive equity curves.
  • To enable cloud sync between your desktop browser and Android mobile app.
  • To authenticate authorized VIP Pro access and process verification OTPs.
  • To protect our community against unauthorized intrusions, bot attacks, and fraudulent activities.
  • To improve platform speed, UI responsiveness, and fix software bugs.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policy

Our website utilizes Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-1815684098843825) to display contextual advertisements:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to this and other websites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
  • Third-party ad servers or networks may also use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and personalize the advertising content you see. Trading Tracker 2026 has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Trading Tracker 2026 uses standard cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, theme selection (Dark/Light mode), and session state. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions between your browser and our cloud servers. Your local data remains under your absolute control and can be exported as an encrypted JSON backup file or wiped at any time using the in-app controls.

6. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (User Rights)

Under international privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA, you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your stored personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request corrections of any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and synced logs.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can export all your trading logs and capital history to Excel (.xlsx) or JSON anytime.

7. Children's Information Protection

Trading Tracker 2026 is designed for adult financial market participants. We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 18. If you believe your child has provided such information on our website, please contact us immediately for swift removal.
