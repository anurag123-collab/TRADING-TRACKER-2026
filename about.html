🎯 Our Mission

In today's fast-moving financial markets, over 90% of retail traders struggle not because of a lack of technical indicators, but due to a lack of discipline, poor risk management, and absent trade journaling.

Trading Tracker 2026 was conceived with a single clear purpose: to bridge the gap between amateur emotion-driven trading and institutional-grade rule-based discipline. By systematically tracking every entry, stop-loss, risk-to-reward ratio, setup tag, and psychological mistake, traders can objectively diagnose their weaknesses and scale their profitability.
